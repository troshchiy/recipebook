# Fruit salad recipe


## Ingredients

- Tuna, canned in water, drained, 1 can
- Green olives, pickled, 50g
- Scallions, raw, 20g
- Jalapeno peppers, raw or pickled, 7g
- Cayenne pepper, 0.5g
- Lemon juice, 2 tbsp
- Olive oil, 1 tbsp
- Romaine lettuce
- Feta cheese, 50g

## Instructions

Any kind of lettuce or mixed greens can replace the Romaine lettuce. Chop olives, finely chop the jalapeno, and dice the lettuce. Combine all of the ingredients except for the lettuce and feta cheese, and then serve over lettuce. Cut feta cheese into cubes and serve on top.
